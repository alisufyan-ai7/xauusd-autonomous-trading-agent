## 3. Proposed v1.1 evidence sufficiency (RECOMMENDATION, not a substituted registration)

1. **Exact narrow scope:** `DEMO_LIFECYCLE_ONLY`, exact admitted demo fingerprint + broker/server/currency/symbol, 0.01-lot cap. For the first bounded lifecycle campaign use one externally verified liquid London–New York-overlap window, with explicit UTC start/end fixed *before* collection. Actual broker session/holiday/event schedule must be obtained independently under separate authority; no hardcoded universal UTC hours.
2. **Minimum observation:** one *contiguous four-hour* qualifying window, with read-only collection across at least 240 UTC-minute bins, >=99% of nominal expected collector checks present and valid, no unclassified data gap or clock conflict, and sufficient genuine timestamped MT5 bid/ask tick pairs to evaluate adverse 1-second, 5-second and 30-second moves **in every eligible ten-minute block**. A sampled poll is not a tick or a simulated fill. Reject sparse/zero-pair blocks rather than interpolate. The 4h/99%/10min proposal is an intentionally explicit preliminary **evidence-sufficiency choice**; it is *not proven* to estimate statistical slippage tails and must be separately adopted before any capture.
3. **Envelope construction:** predeclared worst eligible observed spread and adverse quote moves for BUY/SELL, worst available eligible quote jump and documented adverse gap proxy; sum conservative individually evidenced components, do not use favorable percentiles or retrospectively remove spikes. Include broker/account-specific round-trip commission, holding/swap exposure and conversion with direct evidence or a separately evidenced conservative upper-bound allowance. All units convert to 0.01-lot USD with independently verified direction/asymmetric tick values. If genuine gap or execution-risk proxy is absent/unbounded, **NO_ADMISSION**; do not replace with zero. `current_adverse_cost` and `stressed_adverse_cost` remain pending observed values, with `stressed >= max(2*current, stressed component aggregate)`, ceil to USD cent and object-source SHA binding.
4. **No false bound:** quote movement is only a non-trading *proxy*, not measured fill slippage. Neither the broker's slippage rule nor `max_deviation_points` guarantees stop-loss fill prices. An observed maximum or 2× stress is not a mathematical upper bound; declare residual tail/gap risk, reconcile breaches, quarantine and block new risk. No automatic unknown-position closure.
5. **Live session guard (mandatory before any order):** deny new risk unless server clock/time mapping, live tick age, bid/ask consistency, calibrated spread ceiling, broker stop/freeze parameters, Event Guard/news/shock/session/holiday state and current account/position/order fingerprints are fresh and in the exact admitted set. Proposed freshness threshold is <=2 seconds **only if** independent clock skew is bounded and the threshold is validated offline; otherwise `UNKNOWN` denies. Any extrapolation beyond admitted conditions, including rollover/reopen/weekend, fails closed. No widening the scope on a new day without revalidation of identity, current market guard and calibration source.
6. **Position lifecycle boundary:** **entry lock is not a holding lock**. A stop order can slip and positions can survive into rollover, weekend or events. Therefore an initial owner-attended demo campaign must have an explicitly approved, independently tested position-monitoring, planned-exit, human-only emergency intervention and stale/unknown-exposure protocol *before activation*. Do not assert positions are automatically flattened or that loss is guaranteed <= planned risk. If exposure cannot be reliably reconciled/managed through the boundary, do not activate demo trading.
7. **No full-Policy-A downgrade:** implement and test conservative worst-executable-entry to protective-stop USD loss, ALL verified cost components, normalized stop/volume, same-snapshot account and cashflow history, per-trade cap `round_down_cent(0.0025 * min(balance,equity,10000))`, concurrent/daily/weekly/drawdown/streak checks, V1 and M1; reject if any missing. Keep adverse-price components separate to prevent double counting within `L_plan`. Test far stops, unsupported fee/swap/gap, stale quotes, disallowed periods, failed reconciliation, zero exposure and all 1-cent financial boundaries.
8. **Admissibility and change control:** independently validate immutable raw collector/source SHA-256 and manifest, account-specific contract fees/swap, literal Decimal data, object digest in policy, static collector allow-list, tests and rejected samples. If any criteria fail, **NO_ADMISSION**; never retune on the same evidence. To extend to other sessions or boundaries later, register/collect/validate a separate successor under approval.
