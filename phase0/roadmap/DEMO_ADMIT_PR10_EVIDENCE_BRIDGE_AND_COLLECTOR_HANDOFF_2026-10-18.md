## Admission blockers (do not confuse passing CI with resolved trust)

1. SHA-matched, user-supplied synthetic records and a caller-provided `INDEPENDENTLY_ADMITTED_FOR_REPLAY` label are **not** actual broker account, fee, Event Guard, cost or cashflow attestations. Real binding needs a separately frozen and independently verified provenance root plus full journal reconstruction, latency and account fingerprint binding. An authenticated source bridge does **not** currently exist. `ALLOW_MATH_ONLY` is not order authority.
2. The running demo adapter remains hard **QUARANTINE before order_check/order_send** until approved binding and all frozen Policy A limits are enforced in end-to-end broker-context tests. Do not remove the veto based on this PR's source-pin or simulated evidence.
3. No real four-hour broker quotes, tick values, swaps, commissions, conversion or adverse gap/slippage assumptions have been collected. `current_adverse_cost`/`stressed_adverse_cost` and hashes remain NULL/NOT_ADMITTED. Even a successfully captured quote file would be **private raw evidence only**, not execution-slippage or stop-fill bounds.
4. The current 1s/5s/30s coverage tester uses exact millisecond-offset tick pairs and rejects duplicate-millisecond stamps. Real MT5 ticks may be irregular or share milliseconds. The prospectively registered **timestamp matching tolerance/sequence policy must be explicitly frozen and independently reviewed before any capture**. Do not silently discard adverse quotes or select a favorable interpretation after capture.
5. Broker session/calendar official records, London/NY DST mapping, news/Event Guard/holiday windows, clock skew, supported 4h UTC start/end and owner exact-window authorization are **NOT YET REGISTERED**. If these fail, no capture or admission.
6. The source-hash verification/permit of the future Windows host only checks provided local bytes and matching typed fields; a local static confirmation phrase can be copied and thus cannot serve as cryptographic owner permission. **Separate explicit owner approval and a reviewed allow-listed invocation are required** before any VPS action.
7. A position opened in a restricted session can survive its boundary: an owner-attended monitor, planned exit, human-only emergency and no-automatic-unknown-close behavior must be tested/approved before any demo trading. Event/entry locks cannot guarantee stop fills.
8. Preexisting `mt5_demo._build_request` missing-to-zero `trade_stops_level`, broker quote age guards, and full runtime exposure/cashflow source trust remain open. The new offline bridge does not quietly fix legacy runtime entry logic.
