## Status meanings

- **Accepted:** normative project decision currently in force.
- **Provisionally accepted:** direction is adopted but still requires named evidence before Gate 0 freeze.
- **Proposed:** recommendation awaiting explicit owner approval.
- **Open:** decision or evidence is missing.
- **Rejected:** claim/default must not be used.
- **Superseded:** retained for history and replaced by a later entry.

## Change procedure

Each new entry must state date, status, decision, evidence/rationale, and consequence. A change to an accepted safety, risk, model, broker, data, execution, validation, or deployment decision requires a new entry and a new specification version/hash. Existing entries are never edited to erase history; factual corrections are appended and linked.
