# ADR-2026-10-22-004 — Fast-track a real Exness Pro DEMO pilot; stop repetitive historical gap probes

**Decision type:** Owner priority change / prospective engineering plan. **Status:** PREPARATION ONLY. No broker/API trading approval, no merge or VPS deployment, no change to frozen Policy A, and no LIVE admission.

## Owner priority
The owner requests the simplest safe route to beginning real autonomous **DEMO** order testing, rather than prolonged forensics on nominal missing XAUUSD historical candles. Treat 48-hour trading as an engineering pilot, not proof of profitability or a trigger for real-money admission. The user has not authorized a broker order, Python automated-trading toggle, or activation.

## Evidence that ends the repetitive diagnostic loop
Owner's exact reviewed **read-only** 2026-10-22T14:20:06.510718Z Exness Pro DEMO result reported 564 fully comparable parent buckets, 35 sparse parents and 83 nominal missing lower-timeframe candle slots. All 35 partial OHLC/tick-volume aggregates match available lower bars; no malformed child timestamps; exhaustive 9/9 Sunday D1/H4 parents and 26/26 H4/H1 parents now inventoried. Sunday D1 buckets have one 20:00 UTC H4 child; 21:00 UTC H1 is absent in 26 affected H4 buckets; Friday close and Sunday reopen patterns repeat. Exact evidence: `phase0/evidence/V2_PR34_EXHAUSTIVE_DEMO_READONLY_SPARSE_BUCKETS_2026-10-22.json`; original owner uploaded file SHA256 `6e398fa0bebd9256f63708d658202b2e0c007ba093dee2b21af1e9fa73d9aa63`.

Exness's [instrument-hours page](https://get.exness.help/hc/en-us/articles/4405235684498-Instrument-trading-hours), updated September 29, 2026, says its server uses UTC+0, gold generally closes at rollover, usually 21:00 UTC in summer, and DST/holidays can change schedules. This is high-support **normal-session pattern context**, not full date-by-date historical calendar proof. Historic complete crossframe parity remains **FAIL_CLOSED** and MUST NOT be marked PASS, but that retrospective research classification does **not** itself have to block a narrowly governed future DEMO engineering pilot that independently validates **current** session/quote/account/positions, actual costs, stops, and live-feed integrity.

## Minimal first real DEMO order milestone (separate exact owner permit required)
Use **one deterministic objective TPB variant**, no strategy zoo, no online self-training or AI-driven order sizing. AI Analyst can review/veto only, and its absence may mean NO_TRADE unless an explicit deterministic alternative is accepted later. One eligible setup at a time, only XAUUSD on exact owner-attested Exness Pro DEMO account and server, with `0.01` lot, hard broker protective stop, no averaging or stop widening. Honor all frozen `Policy A`/M1/daily-weekly/streak gates: the owner has not approved higher demo risk. If no valid setups occur, **zero trades is an honest expected possible result**.

Use the existing isolated S2 canary code as a **reference for broker mechanics only** (PR24 remains DRAFT/UNMERGED and S2 staging stays KILLED/UNUSED). It already contains real order-check/send/known-ticket protections for exactly ONE operator-commanded entry/close but **does not autonomously choose a setup, run continuously, or constitute permission to place a DEMO order**. Avoid deploying S2 or silently merging older PR24 into v2. Lift/review only vetted compatibility and deal-reconciliation concepts into a small independently testable DEMO runtime.

## Shortest engineering critical path
1. **Objective trading signal** — close the one TPB variant's required predicates and first-touch tracking on *completed* H4/H1/M15 bars. W1/D1/M5/M1 are context/veto only; do not require complete historic Sunday/H1 gap parity on every old bucket for current entry. Check current session open, fresh bid/ask, current broker bars, spread and no price feed holes **in the active signal/lookback**; NO_TRADE on unknown.
2. **Broker-bound risk + executor** — read exact DEMO identity/mode, reconcile current zero exposure and period cashflows from broker, compute real stop loss and margin with `order_calc_profit`, `order_calc_margin`, `order_check`; apply hard Policy A incl cost stress and SL; journal intent *before* `order_send` and reconcile exact broker fills/positions. Explicitly prevent duplicate submission after timeout/ambiguous ack, quarantine unknown exposure, and never auto-close unidentified positions. Use one 0.01-lot open at a time.
3. **Simple operations** — explicit kill switch, watchdog heartbeat, email+Telegram alert path, deterministic known-position stop/TP management and safe restart/flat confirmation. No unsafe unattended restart or owner-less E1/P1 override.
4. **Admission evidence** — native Windows Python 3.12 synthetic fault tests, one separately authorized **attended real DEMO order** (side/stop selected by completed objective strategy and approved risk governor, not from screenshots), known-position reconciliation/closure, then separately approved **48-hour bounded autonomous DEMO trial** if the runtime and alerts pass. The 48 hours starts only after actual source/ops acceptance, not from this ADR.
5. **LIVE** — separately scoped later capital/risk/security decision; never follows automatically after 48h or from a positive P/L sample.

## What is deferred versus NOT waived
**Deferred for retrospective research:** full source-pinned classification of all 83 historical nominal slots, standalone exhaustive old W1 session reconstruction, broad multi-family strategy optimization and a long untouched OOS program. All historical failed snapshots remain immutable; no backtest profitability can be claimed.

**Cannot defer for the first real DEMO order:** exact account isolation/mode, external-Python trading permission scoped to DEMO, current-session safety, objective source-qualified setup and initial stop, full broker-monetary Policy A checks, protective broker stop, zero/known exposure, durable idempotency/reconciliation, basic alerts/kill, and separate exact owner authorization. Official high-impact event unknown => NO_TRADE, rather than disabling Event Guard. Missing AI transport => NO_TRADE or a separately approved deterministic-only experimental mode, never pretend the model is connected.

## No more duplicate action requests
Do **not** ask owner to rerun the same W1/D1/H4/H1 gap probes just to prepare DEMO runtime. Next work is engineering and controlled validation of a real broker adapter; request at most one targeted owner action when the artifact is exactly pinned and truly actionable. No requirement to manually send screenshots to supply buy/sell for an autonomous strategy.

**Durability:** This ADR supplements ADR-003 without rewriting its fail-closed history. PR32, PR33, PR34 remain DRAFT, production `main` unchanged. No VPS source/task deployment, broker actions, terminal setting toggles or funding carried out by this decision.
