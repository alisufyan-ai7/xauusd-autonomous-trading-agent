## Accepted owner direction — preserve across every new chat

1. Deliver a **continuously operating, independent, AI-assisted Exness Pro DEMO trader** that selects BUY/SELL/NO_TRADE from observed XAUUSD market data; sends genuine orders **to the DEMO broker server** (not simulated or merely one experimental order); reconciles pending tickets, fills, known positions and realized broker results; manages only broker-confirmed positions; and improves strategies through reproducible controlled research.
2. **Opportunity eligibility whenever the actual broker market is tradable**; reject the earlier agent-imposed New York 10:45–11:30 entry whitelist. A **45-minute expiry on one unfilled pending STOP** remains a separate stale-setup safeguard, not a 45-minute daily trading window. Multi-timeframe analysis W1/D1/H4/H1/M15/M5/M1 is driven by completed M15 observations; 5-second polling is for account/order/position supervision, **not** an OpenAI API request every 5 seconds.
3. Use the **existing Windows user environment** `OPENAI_API_KEY`, already configured on the VPS. The owner's actual OpenAI project allowlist is `gpt-5.6-luna`, `gpt-5.6-terra`, `gpt-5.6-sol`; successfully probed/pinned **`gpt-5.6-luna`**. No duplicate secret prompt/file or published keys. Replace agent-imposed **32 AI reviews/day** with a configurable **USD spend budget**, currently default `XAUUSD_OPENAI_DAILY_BUDGET_USD=2.00`, durable per-opportunity cache and spend estimation. This **does not** remove the distinct code's current three **order submissions/day** cap; that is an existing risk/operational setting, not an owner-imposed universal strategy limit, and must be evaluated prospectively with outcome evidence.
4. Position management includes broker-bound protective stop-to-break-even/trailing and **AI-assisted** HOLD/EARLY_EXIT/TIGHTEN_TP assessment for a **known, deal-backed position**. Model cannot invent order size or prices, widen risk, manipulate an unknown position, or blindly retry an uncertain broker write. AI trade decisions and management exist in current source; **first actual live DEMO order/fill, position modify/close still require observation**, not simulated tests. No user per-trade direction approval for the autonomous DEMO service.
5. Record broker-verified outcomes and perform chronological, net-of-cost research with a separate champion/challenger promotion path. This is **not self-training foundation-model weights after each trade**, not an automatically approved new production strategy and not proof of profitability. Retain immutable failures and accepted risk boundaries; never silently revise Policy A or claim historical 0.25% all-cost **cash loss** means 0.25% SL price distance.
6. Keep VPS resource use bounded: deduplicate repetitive `NO_ORDER` cycles, rotate compact `cycles.jsonl` (4 MiB current + three backups), retain durable SQLite trade/risk/AI journals, overwrite compact `health.json`; separately inspect long-running console-log growth and SQLite disk usage. Hidden existing interactive scheduled task, **one writer**; RDP disconnect acceptable, full Windows logoff may end it. Preserve restart/quarantine, incident evidence and separately verify real email/Telegram delivery.
7. Distinguish **DEMO real broker-side lifecycle with virtual funds** from separately consented and qualified **LIVE real-money trading**. Nothing in this decision authorizes LIVE, automatic promotion, unknown-position closure, double-down, or an unbounded risk change. Production software readiness and statistically supported strategy profitability are separate acceptance questions.
