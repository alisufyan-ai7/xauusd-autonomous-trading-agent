## Proposed professional trading intelligence — design outline (NOT implementation acceptance)

1. **Market state:** broker-fed bid/ask and completed multi-timeframe OHLC; deterministic regime/structure pivots and explicit news/session sources. Use D1/W1/H4 as context; H1 zones; M15 trigger; M5/M1 for timing/execution/monitoring. Reject missing or stale data. Synchronize UTC and exclude future candles.
2. **Strategy portfolio:** strategy-versioned and preregistered setup candidates with one active eligible strategy/one position initially; record NO_TRADE and every failed test. Evaluate candidate net economics after spread, commission, swap and adverse slippage/gaps; don't choose profitable-looking backtests after repeatedly tuning holdout.
3. **AI Analyst:** bounded no-tools, no trade-command access, minimal evidence payload, strict schema and state/expiry echo; AI can reject or annotate candidates, NOT authorize risks or expand stops/volume.
4. **Risk control:** policy bound to exact Pro account fingerprint/mode, broker symbol-grid/minimum lot, broker authoritative margin/stop requirements, equity and period cashflows. At $300 and 0.01 lot of a 100oz contract, an adverse $10/oz move is ~$10 price loss (~3.33% before costs): **not a recommended risk limit**. If minimum lot and a structural stop cannot fit owner-approved risk, output NO_TRADE. Never silently activate Standard Cent.
5. **Position management:** independently proposed strategy variant may tighten SL toward BE, trail on causal confirmed swing, or close known position early on invalidation with independently verified idempotency, broker retcode/reconciliation, stop/freeze compliance and outage/unknown-exposure protection. Do NOT widen an active SL; TP management must be tested as a separate exit policy, not an unlogged discretionary override.
6. **Learning:** per-trade rationale, net costs, missed entries and adverse-event/fault labels; performance/reliability drift detectors and challenger replays; one-way process for independently accepting improved strategy+AI prompt versions. Keep deployed LIVE model immutable until separately approved promotion.
7. **Monitoring:** VPS-resident watchdog, per-position reconciliation, heartbeat, risk-loss/drawdown checks and alerts; ChatGPT itself has no direct standing VPS control and cannot claim monitoring when this conversation ends. Produce daily/every-trade structured reports and safe rollbacks.
