# Document Control Policy

- Canonical records live on `main`; downloadable chat bundles are convenience copies.
- Every material change is committed with a clear scope and traceable decision ID where applicable.
- Existing accepted Decision Log entries are never rewritten to conceal history.
- Tags identify frozen checkpoints. Artifact hashes must be recorded before acceptance.
- Draft, proposed, accepted, superseded, withdrawn, and open statuses retain their documented meanings.
- Repository migration does not change Gate status or authorize production implementation.
